The measurable half

NIST CCCBDB experimental fundamentals

Our harmonic frequencies and thermochemistry must land inside a tolerance justified from the literature, or the round failed.

10 molecules verified against NIST CCCBDB. Tolerance: scale harmonics by 0.97, then +/-4% relative or +/-50 cm-1 absolute, whichever is larger. Water and CO2 also carry experimental HARMONIC frequencies - those are compared unscaled.
